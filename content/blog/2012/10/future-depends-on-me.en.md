October fourth was my last day at [Svyaztransneft JSC](http://www.oilnet.ru). I worked there for almost four years, during which I:

* Worked on the development of almost all modules of our enterprise management system in one way or another ("Document Management", "Budgeting", "Contract Management", etc.)
* Developed and improved the system's base classes and components
* Developed tools for automating debugging, testing and other operations
* Set up and maintained a part of the development infrastructure
* Migrated the project to new components, IDE versions, and version control system
* Presented at a Young Specialists competition, where I showed off our team's work on budgeting automation and took first prize.

The time has come to move on. Since October fifth, I've been working at the Moscow branch of [MegaFon](http://www.megafon.ru), where I'm developing a portal using [Django](http://www.djangoproject.com)/[Oracle](http://oracle.com), statistics collection systems, and a number of other tasks.

The main motivation for changing jobs was the opportunity to grow in technologies I love and that are more modern, as my primary occupation, with other conditions being comparable. As far as I can tell, at the new place I'll have more freedom of action and choice in technical decisions — that's also quite significant for me at the moment.

My colleagues and I parted ways amicably and with mutual respect, and I think we'll see each other again more than once when they come to Moscow on business trips ;)
